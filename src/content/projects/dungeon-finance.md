## About

An educational game about economics written in `Rust`. 
A very significant challenge was managing a 48 hour deadline being the sole programmer, 
communicating with two artists, and implementing their assets in the game. 
The game lacks any explanation of how to play due to the time constraints, 
but it is supposed to simulate conditions where users are expected to allocate 
funds efficiently in producing products that meet demand, and engage in 
R&D which improves item quality over the long term, thus satisfying customers, 
and lowering manufacturing cost.
